<div class="article-content">

This video demonstrates an algorithm for registering a 3D foot model using statistical data.

The white model with good topology (manually created) is automatically fitted to a red model obtained through photogrammetry, which has lower-quality topology, a larger number of vertices, and is inconsistent (its structure changes from scan to scan).

- The viewport was developed from scratch in **C++14**, **OpenGL**, and **Qt**.
- The Non-Rigid Alignment algorithm, utilizing Blendshape and nonlinear optimization methods, was implemented in **C++14** and **Eigen**.
